About

I'm a student and self-taught engineer with a habit of chasing problems down to the hardware. That means my work spans notebooks full of model experiments, schematics for logic circuits, and browser tabs full of CSS. I like understanding how something works underneath before I trust myself to build on top of it.

Right now I'm splitting my time between machine learning coursework and projects, VLSI design fundamentals, and front-end web development — treating each as a different lens on the same question: how do you take an idea and turn it into something that actually runs.
