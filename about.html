Background

I'm an Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student with a growing focus on artificial intelligence and machine learning. I like working close to the hardware — understanding how a circuit is laid out, how signals move, how constraints at that level shape what's possible above it — and then bringing that same rigor to software and models.

Most of my project work sits across three areas: training and shipping machine learning models, designing and verifying digital logic in VLSI projects, and building the web tools and interfaces that make the rest of it usable.
